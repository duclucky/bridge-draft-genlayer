BridgeDraft is a bilateral drafting workflow for shared operational plans. A sponsor locks 2 GEN and two named parties submit up to three immutable constraints each. Neither party can start early review until both explicitly complete collection; after the collection deadline, review may proceed without either party blocking it. GenLayer validators judge whether a candidate ASCII draft covers every canonical term and whether terms conflict. Only a balanced result plus both parties' digest-bound ratifications creates two fixed 1 GEN credits. Each party withdraws once through finalized EOA external messages; the verified Studio Dev lifecycle reduced contract balance from 2 to 1 to 0 GEN. Conflict or non-ratification preserves the sponsor refund path. The frontend uses real wallet transactions and canonical reads. A trusted operator supplies candidate generation; the project does not claim legal effect, authenticated model provenance, or proof of offchain performance.
